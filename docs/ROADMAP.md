# Roadmap

## M0 — Core foundation

FastAPI, local generation, study modes, PDF upload, RAG, SSE streaming, and test frontend. Status: substantially complete.

## M1 — Conversation architecture

Explicit IDs, persistent isolated histories, CRUD APIs, streaming synchronization, and frontend conversation controls. Status: complete and tested.

## M2 — Activity tracking

Consent-aware Activity Event Store, followed by subject, topic, and study-time tracking.

## M3 — Learner profile

Explicit preferences, inferred strengths and weaknesses, language preference, and content-depth settings. Manual preferences override inference.

## M4 — Mastery and forgetting

Explainable mastery estimates, review history, and forgetting-risk rules. No medical or psychological diagnosis.

## M5 — Next-best action

Rank useful study actions and attach a human-readable reason to every recommendation.

## M6 — Mentor dashboard

Build actionable Mentor Intelligence views rather than a marks-only dashboard.

## M7 — Adaptive language, depth, and video

Adapt explanations to learner preferences and state. Recommend videos only from trusted sources with valid links and reasons.

## M8 — Community MVP

Add community features without allowing popularity to affect mastery scores.

## M9 — Current affairs, quiz, notes, and planner

Integrate supporting study workflows with the mentor and learner state.

## M10 — Production hardening

Security, authentication, migrations, observability, performance, accessibility, deployment, and comprehensive regression coverage.
